We are a landscaping company based in Jacó, Garabito, operating since 2009. We design, install and maintain green spaces across the Central Pacific — from private gardens to hotel and condo grounds — with irrigation systems that thrive in the coastal climate.

What does our landscaping service include?

Every project is planned around water efficiency and low maintenance. The typical scope includes:

  • Irrigation systems: drip, spray and sprinkler designs adapted to each garden and the local water supply.
  • Automatic watering: programmers, rain sensors and timers that keep your garden green with minimal effort and water waste.
  • Planned landscaping: tropical, low-maintenance designs for residences and hotels that raise curb appeal and property value.
  • Garden maintenance: pruning, fertilizing and pest control for institutional, condo and hotel grounds.

Where we work

From our base in Jacó we coordinate scheduled visits throughout the Central Pacific:

  • Hotels and condos in Jacó, Herradura, Quepos and Manuel Antonio that need dependable green areas.
  • Private gardens and beach properties in Parrita and the surrounding coast.
