We are a construction company based in Jacó, Garabito, operating since 2009. We deliver turnkey general construction: we take responsibility for everything, from plans and permits to handing over the finished project, so you never have to coordinate several contractors yourself.

What does our general construction service include?

Every project is planned with detailed schedules and permanent supervision. The typical scope includes:

  • Foundations and grey works: earthworks, foundations, columns, slabs and masonry with certified concrete and steel.
  • Structure and roofing: steel structures, high-strength roof systems and mezzanines designed for the Pacific climate.
  • Fine finishes: drywall, ceilings, floors, specialized paint and decorative metalwork.
  • Complete permit processing: municipal permits, ICE inspections and approval by the College of Engineers and Architects of Costa Rica (CFIA).

Projects we build

  • Commercial plazas, restaurants and hotels in Quepos and Manuel Antonio.
