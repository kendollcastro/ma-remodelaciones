We are a specialized installation company based in Jacó, Garabito, operating since 2009. We assemble and install everything from commercial furniture to industrial equipment and modular structures across Puntarenas and the Central Pacific, with logistics and functional testing handled by one accountable team.

What does our installation service include?

Every installation follows a documented process so your equipment or structure operates correctly from day one:

  • Commercial furniture: assembly and placement of furniture and fixtures for offices, restaurants, stores and hotels.
  • Industrial equipment: positioning, anchoring and connection of machinery, with coordination of certified electrical work.
  • HVAC systems: installation and testing of air conditioning and ventilation units for residential and commercial spaces.
  • Modular structures: assembly of prefabricated and modular buildings, containers and temporary facilities.

A process you can rely on

We manage every stage so your installation arrives on time and works correctly:

  • Logistics: delivery coordination and on-site preparation, from our base in Jacó out to Puntarenas, Quepos, Parrita and beyond.
  • On-site assembly: specialized crews with the right tools, safety standards and certified technicians.
  • Functional testing and handover: we test each installation and hand over with documentation, so there are no surprises.
