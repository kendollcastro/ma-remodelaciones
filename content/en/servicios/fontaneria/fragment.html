We are a plumbing company based in Jacó, Garabito, operating since 2010. We design and install complete hydraulic systems across the Central Pacific — from storm and sanitary drainage to potable water — and we fix leaks fast, so your property stays dry and compliant.

What does our plumbing service include?

We handle the whole water system of your home or business, new build or remodel:

  • Complete hydraulic systems: potable water lines, storm drainage and sanitary systems, designed and installed to code.
  • Leak detection and repair: locate and fix hidden leaks in walls, floors and underground lines without unnecessary demolition.
  • Hydro-pneumatic pumps: pressure systems that guarantee steady water flow for homes, hotels and businesses.
  • Water heaters and drainage: installation of water heaters and complete drainage systems, tested before handover.

  • Hotels, restaurants and commercial buildings in Manuel Antonio, Puntarenas and Parrita.
