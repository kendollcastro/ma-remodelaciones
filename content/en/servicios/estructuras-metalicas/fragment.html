We are a steel structures company based in Jacó, Garabito, operating since 2009. We design, fabricate and assemble steel structures across the Central Pacific — from industrial warehouses and galeras to roofs, mezzanines and large trusses — built to withstand the region's humid, saline air.

What does our steel structure service include?

We take your structure from design to final assembly with certified materials and supervised workmanship:

  • Design: structural calculation and detailing for warehouses, roofs, mezzanines and large trusses.
  • Certified fabrication: cutting, welding and painting with certified structural steel and international welding standards.
  • Assembly: on-site erection with qualified crews, safety protocols and precise crane coordination.
  • Pacific-climate resistant: anti-corrosive protection designed to resist saline air and humidity.

Additional metalwork

Beyond large structures, we fabricate and install functional and decorative metalwork:

  • Stairs and railings for residential and commercial projects.
  • Walkways, platforms and access structures for industrial facilities.
