What does our maintenance service include?

Our technicians cover all the daily needs of residential and commercial properties:

  • Plumbing and pumps: leak repairs, hydro-pneumatic pumps and water pressure solutions.
  • Sewage systems: septic tanks, drainage and sewage maintenance that keeps your property hygienic and compliant.
  • Facade cleaning: washing, waterproofing touch-ups and care for exterior surfaces exposed to humid coastal air.
  • Miscellaneous repairs: doors, hardware, tiles, fixtures and any detail that keeps your property in top condition.
