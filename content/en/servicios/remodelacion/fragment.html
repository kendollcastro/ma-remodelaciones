We are a remodeling company based in Jacó, Garabito, operating since 2010. We transform commercial and residential spaces across the Central Pacific, raising the value of your property with premium finishes and careful planning that keeps disruption to a minimum.

What does our remodeling service include?

Every project is planned in phases with clear schedules and permanent supervision. The typical scope includes:

  • Drywall divisions: interiors, acoustic walls and ceilings that give new life to any space.
  • Exterior facades: cladding, textures and restoration that protect and renew your building envelope against saline air.
  • Specialized paint: anti-humidity, anti-corrosive and decorative finishes for the Pacific climate.
  • Ceilings, floors and decorative finishes: from floating floors and tile to decorative millwork that completes the look.

Spaces we transform

Our experience extends across the province of Puntarenas:

  • Homes, condos and vacation rentals in Jacó, Herradura and Playa Hermosa.
  • Restaurants, hotels and shops in Quepos and Manuel Antonio.
  • Offices, warehouses and workshops in Puntarenas and Parrita.

One contractor, one warranty

We coordinate all trades in-house, including electrical and plumbing, so you deal with a single team led by Robert Matarrita, founder and CEO. All work is guaranteed: from 3 months for repairs to 24 months for major works.
