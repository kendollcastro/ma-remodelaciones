Jacó is the tourist and residential heart of Costa Rica's Central Pacific, with a growing town, an expanding hotel scene and beach communities in nearby Herradura and Playa Hermosa. From our base right here, MA Soluciones has been building in this area since 2010.

What we build in Jacó

With more than 300 projects behind us, our crew handles every scope in and around Jacó:

  • Homes and vacation rentals: turnkey construction from grey works to fine finishes in Jacó, Herradura and Playa Hermosa.
  • Hotels and condos: new builds, remodels and maintenance that keep hospitality properties operating at full occupancy.
  • Businesses: commercial fit-outs, restaurants and small plazas for Jacó's active commercial scene.

Built for the beach climate

Coastal properties face saline air and high humidity, so we work with certified materials that resist corrosion and moisture — from steel protection systems to anti-humidity paint and marine-grade fixtures. Every project is guaranteed: from 3 months for repairs to 24 months for major works.
