Parrita is the agricultural-coastal canton that sits between Jacó and Quepos, where farmland, palm plantations and rural communities meet the Central Pacific coast. Properties here need practical construction and dependable maintenance that stand up to a warm, humid climate.

How we serve Parrita

From our base in Jacó, we bring the full MA Soluciones service to the canton:

  • Landscaping and irrigation: efficient watering systems, automatic programmers and garden maintenance for homes and institutions.
  • Maintenance and repairs: plumbing, pumps, facade care and general fixes that keep rural and coastal properties in good shape.
  • Construction: homes, warehouses and small commercial projects built turnkey with certified materials.

Built for the coastal climate

With saline air and heavy seasonal rains, we select corrosion-resistant materials, protective coatings and moisture-proof finishes for every job in Parrita.
