Jacó receives guests year-round, and in vacation rentals a five-star review is won — or lost — on details the guest sees in the first 10 minutes: the bathroom floor, water pressure, garden lighting at night, and the roof when it rains. This is the checklist we use with our villa operator clients in Jacó to get every property ready before high season and between stays.

Checklist before every season

  • Water and plumbing: check leaks under sinks and toilets, water heaters, shower pressure and preventive drain clearing (hair and sand clog fast here).
  • Electrical: test outdoor outlets, garden lights, fans and air conditioning; verify the panel and breakers.
  • Roofs and seals: before the rainy season, inspect sheets, gutters and window seals. A small leak ruins a drywall ceiling in one weekend.
  • Floors and walls: touch-up high-traffic scuffs, tile grout lines and bathroom silicone (old blackened silicone shows up in listing photos).
  • Garden and exteriors: lawn mowing, palm trimming, path lighting and pool cleaning. The exterior is the first photo of your listing.

Between stays: the maintenance that protects your income

Turnover work is not just cleaning: it is the cheap window to fix small things before they become big ones. A dripping faucet, a loose tile or a burnt fixture repaired between stays costs a fraction of solving it with the guest already inside — and it prevents reviews that drop your occupancy for months. Many of our Jacó villa clients run a preventive maintenance plan with scheduled visits that does exactly this.

If the owner lives outside Costa Rica

Distance does not have to be a problem, but it does require method: a single point of contact, photo and video reports after every visit, and decisions approved in writing before execution. At MA Soluciones we routinely work with Jacó owners living in the United States or San José: we schedule inspections, perform the fixes and send before-and-after evidence.
